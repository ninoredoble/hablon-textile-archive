# HABLON

> **Indigenous Mindanao Textile Geometry & Vector Loom Archive**  
> A digital cultural archive and generative vector engine preserving the sacred mathematical weaving patterns of Mindanao—including T'boli T'nalak, Yakan Seputangan, Maguindanao Inaul, and Maranao Landap.

---

## 🏛️ Overview

Mindanao’s indigenous backstrap weaving traditions encode sophisticated mathematical symmetry groups, dihedral geometries, and fractal permutations executed entirely through mental arithmetic without written templates.

**HABLON** translates these oral geometric archives into open-source vector algorithms. Designed in the aesthetic tradition of fine art museum catalogues, it provides researchers, cultural educators, and designers with interactive tools to explore cultural provenance, adjust warp/weft densities, and export production-ready vector SVGs.

---

## 🧵 Documented Traditions & Symmetry Groups

1. **T'nalak (T'boli &bull; Lake Sebu, South Cotabato)**:
   - *Symmetry*: $D_4$ Dihedral Diamond Lattice.
   - *Fibers & Dyes*: Boiled bark tannins (black), wild ginger roots (red), and beeswax-burnished abaca (*Musa textilis*).
2. **Seputangan (Yakan &bull; Lamitan, Basilan)**:
   - *Symmetry*: $P_{4m}$ Wallpaper Group (Stepped fractal rhombus and python diamond motifs).
   - *Technique*: Supplementary weft on five-harness backstrap tension looms.
3. **Inaul (Maguindanao &bull; Sultan Kudarat)**:
   - *Symmetry*: $P_m$ Alternating chevron vectors (*Binaludtu* / rainbow waves).
   - *Materials*: Mulberry silk interlocked with metallic lurex thread.
4. **Landap & Okir (Maranao &bull; Lake Lanao)**:
   - *Symmetry*: $C_2$ Longitudinal vector bands (*Lakit*) combined with curvilinear spiral scrolls.

## 🎨 Architectural Standards

- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist gallery catalog inspired by Japanese textile archives and Scandinavian museum folios.
- **Color Palette**: Gallery white (`#fbfbfa`), charcoal ink (`#121212`), raw terracotta thread (`#a8432a`), and natural unbleached ecru (`#eeebe5`). Zero artificial glowing halos or saturated gradients.
- **Typography**: Classical editorial serifs (`Cinzel`) paired with technical grotesque captions (`Inter` and `JetBrains Mono`).
- **Core Engine**: Real-time procedural SVG synthesis with 1-click vector asset download.
